TRENDNOVA STARTER WEBSITE
=========================

FILES
- index.html: home page, category filters, search, story cards and ad placeholders
- style.css: responsive dark editorial design
- script.js: search, category filtering, article dialogs and mobile navigation
- about.html, privacy.html, contact.html: starter policy/info pages

RUN LOCALLY
Open index.html in a browser. For local development, you can also use a simple local server.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Change TrendNova to your chosen brand name if desired.
2. Replace starter/sample articles with your own original, accurate, useful content.
3. Replace the example contact email in contact.html with an email you control.
4. Review and customize the privacy policy to reflect your real site, analytics and advertising tools.
5. Test all pages on mobile and desktop.
6. Add only the exact ad code generated in your HilltopAds publisher dashboard after the website has been accepted. Replace the two ad placeholder blocks in index.html.
7. Do not use fake traffic, forced redirects, misleading download buttons, unauthorized copyrighted downloads or content you do not have rights to publish.

FREE HOSTING OPTIONS
- GitHub Pages: https://pages.github.com/
- Vercel: https://vercel.com/
You can begin with a free provider subdomain; a custom domain is optional at first.

IMPORTANT
This is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript starter. Article content is stored in script.js, so adding many articles requires editing that file. A content management system or backend can be added later if you need easier publishing.
